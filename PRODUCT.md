# OmniDome Telecom Cloud OS

## Product Overview
OmniDome is an AI-native operational platform and Cloud Operating System for Fibre Internet Service Providers (ISPs), Alternative Network Operators (AltNets), and Telecommunications enterprises in South Africa and emerging markets.

## Target Audience
- **Executive Leadership & Operations Directors**: Need real-time MRR trends, network health, FNO SLAs, churn prevention, and corporate strategy compliance (OKRs, BCEA wellness rules).
- **Customer Care & Call Center Agents**: Manage high-velocity billing disputes, optical line faults (LOS, PON), speed complaints, and customer 360 profiles.
- **Sales & Retention Specialists**: Require automated lead feasibility checks, coverage maps, discount approval matrices, and churn-risk interventions.
- **Field Sales & Network Technicians**: Require mobile-first apps for on-site feasibility, RICA document capture, ONT installations, and fiber splicing validation.
- **End Consumers & Business Clients**: Access self-service customer portals, guest sign-in, invoice downloads, data top-ups, Wi-Fi management, and promotional landing pages.

## Core Problem Solved
Traditional ISP operations rely on fragmented legacy software (separate billing, CRM, radius/BRAS, network NMS, and ticketing silos). OmniDome unifies telemetry, billing, CRM, network monitoring, and human-in-the-loop AI orchestration into a single sovereign platform with zero vendor lock-in.

## Operating Context & Market Specifics
- **Currency & Billing**: South African Rand (ZAR / R), VAT calculations, Paystack & EFT payment rails.
- **Network Topologies**: Major South African FNOs (Vumatel, Openserve, Frogfoot, MetroFibre, Octotel).
- **Compliance & Regulation**: Strict POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) privacy guards and RICA identity verification before line activation.
- **Socio-Economic Inclusivity (Ubuntu Principle)**: Human-centric staff wellness protections (BCEA shift caps) and transparent customer communication.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Technical & Authoritative**: Confident, data-backed, precise telecommunications engineering vocabulary.
- **Empathetic & Human**: Courteous, constructive, and de-escalating during network faults or billing queries.
- **Crisp & Operational**: High signal-to-noise ratio; actionable executive summaries rather than generic conversational filler.
