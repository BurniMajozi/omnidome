What can a plan afford to deliver?

Subscription, AI, research, hosting and payment costs in one editable model. This is a design calculator, not a connected billing screen. Delivery costs are illustrative until measured. All revenue and delivery-cost inputs are in Rand, excluding recoverable VAT.

Commercial assumptions

For annual plans use discounted monthly net revenue and 1/12 collections per month. This models gateway fees allocated across the service period; it does not model monthly cash flow. Fee VAT is assumed recoverable, and the merchant's actual gateway agreement should replace these assumptions.

Delivery assumptions

AI/research unit costs must include their own model, tool and normal execution cost. Extra runtime is only additional execution not already counted above. Avoid counting it twice.

Credit count alone says nothing about delivery cost. A weighted rate card must explain how many credits each operation uses.
